## Long Story Short

As a parent of a 2-year-old, the question is never which park. It's who else will be there. A great park with no other kids is a fifteen-minute visit. A shitty park with a friend is two hours. Picking the right park is a social decision, not a logistics one.

## UX Decisions

Three calls that shaped everything else — each shown as a real fragment of the app, with the *why* annotated:

<ScoutPrivacy />

Sharing a *sentence* — never a live location dot — was the single most important call. Audience is a first-class control inside the compose flow, not buried in a settings page, and every beacon auto-expires so there's no permanent record of where a kid plays.

<ScoutType />

All-bold sans reads like a system alert — every word weighted the same, productivity register. The same SF Pro family in regular weight, with italic + pink colour on *with a friend*, makes the sentence whisper its point instead of shouting it. The face stays system-native; the emotion lives in the italic.

<ScoutButton />

The primary CTA is thumb-sized and full-width because the user has a toddler on one hip when they tap it. Calling it "Wave & join" instead of "Add friend" lowers the stakes — you're posting a beacon, not a permanent relationship.

## Core Flow

<CoreFlowGrid>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Discover" title="The map">Park pins show who from your circle is there right now. A pulse ring marks any park with an active beacon from someone you know.</CoreFlowStep>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Decide" title="Park detail">Crowd now plus a 24-hour chart so you can plan around nap windows. Buddies surface as named cards. Community reports from the last 24 hours.</CoreFlowStep>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Signal" title="Drop a beacon">Tap the park, pick a time, set your audience. Posts as a sentence: "I'm heading to Guitar Playground at 4." Expires automatically.</CoreFlowStep>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Plan" title="Compose a playdate">Park, time, audience — the whole thing reads like a text message. Visibility is a first-class control, not a settings page.</CoreFlowStep>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Report" title="Community report">Three categories, one sentence, severity. Cleanliness, maintenance, or a heads-up. Reports expire after 24 hours.</CoreFlowStep>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Match" title="Find a buddy">Age-window matching explained inline. Wave and join becomes a beacon, not a friend request.</CoreFlowStep>
</CoreFlowGrid>

## Visual Direction

Warm, edible, neighbourly — the "Sorbet" palette. Aurora-style backgrounds melt peach into pink into lavender; deep plum ink keeps the text grounded so the gradients never feel sugary. SF Pro Display sets a system-native voice, with italic + colour doing the emotional emphasis instead of a separate display face.

## Important to know

**Trust.** Sharing intent, not GPS. GPS time expire. Audience scope visible at every post. The privacy designed in the app, not thru the settings section.
