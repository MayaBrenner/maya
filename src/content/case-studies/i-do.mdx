## Long Story Short

Couples plan weddings across seven different tools — WhatsApp, Google Sheets, a notes app, vendor PDFs, email chains, a seating website, and a budget spreadsheet. None of them talk to each other. The result is a fragmented, stressful process that turns a joyful event into a project management nightmare.

## Research

I spoke with recently married couples and wedding planners about how they actually managed the process.

## Core Flow

<CoreFlowGrid>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Setup" title="Onboarding">Names, date, venue, budget, partner invite — a five-step wizard that populates the whole workspace. Done in under three minutes.</CoreFlowStep>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Overview" title="Dashboard">Mission control. Days until the wedding, budget status, tasks due this week, RSVP count, and vendor progress — the whole picture at a glance.</CoreFlowStep>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Planning" title="Timeline">A roadmap, not a calendar. Milestones grouped by how far out they are — 12 months, 6 months, wedding week — with completion tracked visually.</CoreFlowStep>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Coordination" title="Vendor workspace">Each vendor gets their own page: contract status, payments, contacts, notes, and the open action item. No more digging through email threads.</CoreFlowStep>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Logistics" title="Guests and seating">A CRM for everyone you love. RSVP tracking, dietary notes, plus a drag-and-drop seating chart with live conflict checks.</CoreFlowStep>
  <CoreFlowStep phase="Day-of" title="Mobile runbook">On the wedding day, everyone — planner, photographer, caterer — is on their phone. The runbook goes dark and mobile: a shared, live schedule designed for glances in low light.</CoreFlowStep>
</CoreFlowGrid>

## Visual Direction

Calm, warm, and deliberately unhurried. The palette draws from champagne and cream — luxe without being loud. EB Garamond brings an editorial quality to headings; Hanken Grotesk keeps the UI crisp and legible. The goal was to feel like a beautifully printed invitation, not a SaaS dashboard.
