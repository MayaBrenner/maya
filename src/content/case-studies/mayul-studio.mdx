## Overview

Mayul Studio is a premium stationery brand I founded in Tel Aviv. This case study documents the brand identity I built from scratch — the decisions behind every visual element, and how the system scales across products, packaging, and digital channels.

## The Challenge

Starting a brand means making foundational decisions that will represent your work for years. The challenge was distilling a design philosophy — precision, warmth, and quiet luxury — into a visual language that works on a 5cm envelope corner and a full-page Instagram post alike.

<Callout type="insight">
A brand identity for a stationery company has a unique constraint: the product *is* design. Every customer touches the brand. The identity can't be decoration — it has to be design at the same level as the product itself.
</Callout>

## Process

<ProcessTimeline>
  <Step phase="Research" title="Competitive landscape">
    Mapped the premium stationery market — Rifle Paper Co., Papier, and local Israeli brands — to identify white space in the visual language.
  </Step>
  <Step phase="Strategy" title="Brand positioning">
    Defined Mayul Studio's positioning: quiet, considered luxury with a distinctly local character. Not loud. Not trendy. Enduring.
  </Step>
  <Step phase="Identity" title="Logo and mark">
    Developed a wordmark and secondary mark system that works at scale — from embossing on packaging to a 16px favicon.
  </Step>
  <Step phase="System" title="Guidelines and rollout">
    Documented the full system: color values, type scale, spacing, usage rules, and examples across all applications.
  </Step>
</ProcessTimeline>

## Visual Identity

The palette centers on warm neutrals — not the cold whites of mass-market stationery, but the ivory of good paper stock. The accent color is used sparingly, the way a wax seal punctuates a letter.

Typography pairs an editorial serif (for product names and editorial moments) with a clean, unobtrusive sans (for product codes, labels, and UI).

## Outcome

Mayul Studio launched and grew into an internationally shipping brand, distributed across stores in Tel Aviv and available online. The brand identity held across every touchpoint — which is the only real test of a brand system.
